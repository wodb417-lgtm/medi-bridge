Clinic interpreter and AI scribe

Demo guide

An interpreter when the patient speaks another language.
A chart note when the visit is in Korean.

Those are two different jobs, so they live on separate screens. Open the three views in the order below and it is clear which one does what.

How to look at it

  1. 1
    Open the clinician screen and the patient screen side by side.

    Keep Interpreter in this window. Open the patient tablet in another window, or on a tablet.

  2. 2
    Press Speak as clinician and talk in Korean.

    The translation shows up on the patient screen, and the audio plays one sentence at a time. Choose the patient’s language on the clinician screen.

  3. 3
    Create the note on the scribe screen.

    Record the visit on AI scribe, then press Create visit note. Warnings and instructions the clinician gave are pinned at the top, in red.
